How coins work

  • 1 ₹ = 1 coin. Add ₹10 to ₹99,999 to your wallet by UPI.
  • Coins are added after the shop checks your UPI payment (UTR number). Usually within a few hours during shop timings.
  • Lucky Spin: 1 paid spin = 10 coins. The 10th paid spin wins a free headphones gift (with any product purchase).
  • Mobile recharge: e.g. ₹349 recharge = 349 coins.
  • Buy a mobile: e.g. a ₹15,000 mobile = 15,000 coins.
  • If the shop rejects a recharge / purchase request, your coins are returned to the wallet.
  • Coins are for use in SM Mobile Deals only. They cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred. Terms apply.
